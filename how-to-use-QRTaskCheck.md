# How to Schedule Inspection Tasks in Advance

## Quick Overview
Scheduling tasks ahead of time allows your team to prepare upcoming location checks for future dates without cluttering your daily field work. By setting an inspection date in advance, all assigned locations remain safely on standby—with no running countdown timers and zero risk of accidental early scanning—until the scheduled day arrives.

---

## Before You Begin
Before scheduling a task, make sure you have:
* **Admin Permissions**: You must be logged into QR Task Check with an Administrator account.
* **Available Locations**: The physical locations you want to check must be active and set up under the matching Task Type (e.g., Pest Control or Flying Insects).
* **Target Inspection Date**: Have your intended inspection date in mind (you can schedule any date from tomorrow onwards).

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Open the Task Manager
From the main navigation sidebar on the left side of your screen, click **Task Manager**.

### 2. Start a New Task
In the top-right corner of the Task Manager screen, click the **Create Task** button. This will open the **Create New Task** window.

> `Clicking the Create Task button on the Task Manager page`

### 3. Enter the Task Name
In the **Task Name** field, type a clear and recognizable name for your inspection (e.g., *"PESTCON"*).

### 4. Select the Task Type
Under **Task Type**, click the card that matches your inspection category. (Treatment or Monitoring)
* Once selected, the available locations for that category will automatically load in the right-hand panel.

### 5. Pick Your Future Schedule Date
Click the **Schedule Date** box (which defaults to today's date). A calendar will appear:
1. Use the left and right arrows to find your desired month.
2. Click on the future date you want the inspection to take place.
3. The selected date will now display in the schedule box (e.g., `09/07/2026`).

> `Selecting a future date using the calendar picker`

### 6. Choose Locations to Inspect
In the **Select Locations** panel on the right:
* Check the individual boxes next to the locations you want your team to inspect.
* *Need to select everything?* Check the **Select All** box at the top of the list.
* You can also use the **Search locations…** box to quickly find specific rooms or checkpoints.

### 7. Save and Schedule
Review your selected locations count at the bottom-left of the window, then click **Create Task**.
* Your new task is now created and will appear in the Task Manager with a blue **Scheduled** badge showing its upcoming date.

> `Created task showing the blue Scheduled badge`

## Pro-Tip: Adding Extra Locations Later
> [!TIP]
> **Need to add more checkpoints to an upcoming task?**  
> Simply click **View** next to any scheduled task on your Task Manager table, then select your additional locations from the overview window. The system will automatically schedule those new locations for the exact same future date—no need to re-enter the calendar date!

## Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### Why can't field workers see the task on their QR Scanner?
This is completely normal and intentional! Future-scheduled tasks stay strictly inactive until their scheduled calendar date arrives. This keeps your field workers' scan lists clean and focused only on today's active work. Once the clock strikes 12:00 AM on the scheduled date, the task will automatically activate and appear in the scanner.

### Will the 24-hour countdown timer start running immediately?
No. Countdown timers stay paused and inactive until the actual scheduled date arrives. When that day comes, your team receives the full 24-hour window from the start of the scheduled date to complete every check.

### What happens if an operator scans a QR code early by mistake?
If a worker scans a physical QR code for a location assigned to a future task, QR Task Check will display an informational notice explaining that the location is scheduled for an upcoming date and cannot be started yet. No checklists will be opened and no accidental records will be created.
